01 / About

個人簡介About Me

我於2025年取得國立中山大學企業管理博士學位，主修行銷。研究主要關注消費者行為與消費心理，以實驗設計與實證方法探討情境線索、心理反應及其行為後果。

I earned my Ph.D. in Business Administration, specializing in marketing, from National Sun Yat-sen University in 2025. My research focuses on consumer behavior and psychology, using experimental and empirical methods to examine contextual cues, psychological responses, and behavioral outcomes.

我的學術訓練涵蓋工程、管理與行銷，並具備十餘年專利工程師經驗。2024至2025年間，曾赴加拿大 Simon Fraser University 的 Beedie School of Business 進行訪問研究。希望透過跨領域視角，連結學術理論、數據分析與企業實務。

My training spans engineering, management, and marketing, complemented by over a decade of experience as a patent engineer. From 2024 to 2025, I conducted visiting research at the Beedie School of Business, Simon Fraser University, Canada. I seek to connect academic theory, data analysis, and industry practice through an interdisciplinary perspective.
